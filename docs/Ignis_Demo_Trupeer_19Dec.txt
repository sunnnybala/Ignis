Hello. Welcome to this demo of Ignis, an AI assistant that efficiently guides researchers from an initial idea to a fully developed research paper. Let's take it for a spin with some vision transformer research.
Once you submit an idea, Ignis kicks things off by refining it to ensure clarity and focus.
Next, Ignis evaluates the idea's novelty by conducting a comprehensive literature review. It then progresses to crafting an implementation plan, assists in coding, runs the experiments when you’re set, and neatly stores the results ready for paper creation.
With these results in hand, Ignis generates a research paper that is ready for publication. Additionally, it provides a publication probability and a list of suggested journals. You have the option to request modifications based on your feedback.
Ignis also makes sure that the paper adheres to the required format and template of the chosen journal, while offering an acceptance probability for that specific journal.
